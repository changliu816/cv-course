Program Overview

This is a 5-month program. The first phase builds your deep learning foundations through coursework. The second phase transitions you into active research — starting with a structured onboarding project, then opening up to independent research if you're ready.

Program Timeline

📚
Phase 1 · Month 1 – 1.5

Coursework & Assignments

Work through the 3 course modules and complete all 3 assignments. This gives you the foundation in deep learning — CNNs, optimization, transformers — needed for the research phase.


View Modules →  ·  View Assignments →
🔬
Phase 2 · Month 1.5 – 5

Research — Robust Fine-Tuning (Onboarding)

Start with the RFT onboarding project: read two papers on robust fine-tuning, then reproduce the results using the provided notebook. This is designed to teach you how to read papers, run experiments, and structure findings.


Go to Research Track →
🚀
Phase 3 · For Advanced Students

Independent Research Topics

If you complete the onboarding project ahead of schedule, or already have a strong background, you are encouraged to propose and explore your own research direction. Discuss with the instructor to define a topic and plan.


Contact instructor to discuss →
