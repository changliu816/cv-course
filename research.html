Background

Large pretrained vision-language models (e.g. CLIP) achieve strong zero-shot accuracy across diverse data distributions. However, standard fine-tuning on a specific target dataset improves in-distribution performance while often hurting robustness to distribution shifts.

This research track studies two methods that address this tradeoff — and asks you to reproduce one of them on the iWildCam benchmark.

Step 1 — Read the Papers

Read both papers before working on the reproduction notebook.
